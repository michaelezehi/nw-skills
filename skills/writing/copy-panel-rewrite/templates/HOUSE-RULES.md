# House rules every panel works within

These are fixed by the owner. A panel can choose between versions or write
its own, but its text must obey these.

- The product is <name>. It <what it does, for whom, in one sentence>. <Release
  date or reason for the sweep.>
- <Naming rules: what our things are called and never called.>
- No em dashes or en dashes, and no spaced hyphen used as a dash. Straight
  quotes. <Spelling variety and key spellings.>
- <Words never used in user copy.>
- <If narrated:> Questions, intros and recaps are read aloud, so no slashes,
  no parentheses, no "e.g.".
- <If scored:> Answer options are scored. A replacement keeps the meaning of
  the option it replaces, and a set keeps its order and count. A packet item
  lists any keyword that must stay.
- Do not promise anything the product does not do.
