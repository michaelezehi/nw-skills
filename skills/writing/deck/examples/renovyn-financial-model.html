RENOVYN · 09RECONCILIATION
Operating shape · matches deck slide 16
Where the £1.5M lands. The deck and the model agree.
Marketing, community & partnerships
50%
£750,000
Mental Health Awareness Week launch, lived-experience content engine, NHS / GamCare / SMART outreach, social-media and PR. The four marketing-weighted hires sit here, plus the £260k campaign budget.
Engineering & product
25%
£375,000
One senior engineer, designer share, native modules ownership, AISP integration, RCT preparation, infrastructure. The platform is built; this layer ships the unblocks.
Compliance, advisory & reserve
25%
£375,000
DPIA, Article 9 health-data legal counsel, advisor stipends (Joseph + clinical + recovery sector + brand + capital), FX buffer, contingency.
Why marketing-weighted
The platform is built. With Joseph as CEO and Michael as founder, the gap is reach, not capability. We need a content engine, partnership credibility, and a Mental Health Awareness Week launch that turns visibility into trust. One engineer ships the unblocks; four marketing-weighted hires build the engine.
Why advisors over hires
Higher-level help comes through five named advisor slots: clinical, recovery sector, brand, capital, plus Joseph in the CEO + advisor flow. Token equity and modest stipends. Senior credibility for ~4% of the raise instead of full-time salaries.
Reconciliation: Marketing & community = Marketing + GTM + Social Media + Community + PR + Designer + comms-share of leadership. Engineering = Senior Engineer + Tech & infra + eng-share of founder + designer build time. Compliance & advisory = G&A + Advisor stipends + Reserve + leadership-share.
